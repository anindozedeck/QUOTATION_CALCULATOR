# Client delivery message: Quotation Calculator

Hello,

Your quotation calculator is ready. Open `QUOTATION_CALCULATOR.html` in a modern web browser to prepare an estimate: enter the job type, material cost, labour days, and daily labour rate, then select **Calculate quotation**. The calculator displays the materials and labour amounts, applies 16% VAT, and shows the estimated total in KES. It runs locally, with no installation, server, or internet connection required.

`README.md` includes the setup, usage, and calculation details. Please verify that the fixed VAT rate and whole-shilling rounding are appropriate for your business before issuing a formal quotation.

**Revision vs. new feature:** A revision would change an existing behavior or presentation (such as adjusting the VAT rate or labels), while a new feature would add a capability the calculator does not currently have (such as saving or exporting quotations).
